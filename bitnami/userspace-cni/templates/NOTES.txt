  - A userspace switch (OVS-DPDK or VPP) must already be installed and running on

  - Multus-CNI (or another meta-plugin) is required to invoke the `userspace` binary
    as a secondary network attachment via NetworkAttachmentDefinition. userspace-cni
    is not a primary cluster CNI.
